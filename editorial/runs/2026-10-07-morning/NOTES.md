a new consequence. The Agency for Civil Protection said the CPR number can no longer be used to identify
anyone. The ministry said an unusually large invoice gave the breach away, and pharmacies tightened their
checks. Six scoring outlets carried it, and it ranks first on breadth and thread. The pharmacies cluster
is folded into the lead as a source, not run as its own brief.

**Secondaries.** The opening speech: seven outlets, with Russia and the town-country divide as its spine.

Gråsten kidnapping link and because the page needed a foreign story with a Danish hook. The 900 million
for schools, the first hard number from the finance bill.

Kærshovedgård, the legislative programme, the SU defence, public debt, Vipps, Genmab, Red Bull, car
seats, the LGBTQ+ handbook, the Hammerum arrest (a development on yesterday's brief) and Lise Svanholm.

the handbook caps a section at half the page.
