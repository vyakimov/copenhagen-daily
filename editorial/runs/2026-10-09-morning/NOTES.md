The window held 4,092 articles over 72 hours ending at 05:30. All 132 feeds at 17 publishers polled.

**Lead: the senior pension cut in the leaked finance bill.** Jyllands-Posten, Berlingske, Børsen and Ritzau all had it by Thursday evening, and unions, BUPL and Ældre Sagen were protesting within hours. It changes a benefit for worn-out workers, and it is the sharpest new fact in the finance-bill thread. The ranking put it first, and I agree. The bill is formally presented after the cutoff, so the copy has to treat it as a leak.
