# Editorial log: Wednesday 14 October 2026, morning edition

The window held 3,062 articles from 05:30 on Sunday to 05:30 this morning. All 132 feeds at 17 publishers were polled. It is the autumn holiday, and the Danish news was thin. The Danish publishers carried a lot of small domestic items, and the section weights pushed them up the ranking.

**Lead: the cyber incident at Femern.** The ranking put it third, behind Copenhagen flat prices and the motor register. I put it first. It hit the state's largest construction project, five outlets carried it, and by evening the minister for civil preparedness had summoned the parties behind the cyber strategy. One cluster holds both the incident and the summons, the way the golden lead held incident and response. Nobody has said who was behind the attack. The copy must not guess.

**Secondaries.** Flat prices, because six outlets carried them and a falling Copenhagen market is a consequence readers feel. The Aarhus University calculation error, because it changes a regulation that farmers are bound by. The three transplant surgeons sent home from Rigshospitalet, because the Danish Health Authority has stepped in. Two world departures: the Pope's surgery, where the new fact is the cancer diagnosis (the surgery itself ran on the 12th), and the Riksdag vote on Andersson, which moves the Swedish thread forward. Each was carried by six Danish outlets but ranked in the sixties on world weight.

**Briefs.** The motor register was ranked second but was back the same afternoon, so it runs as a brief. The rest of the briefs were chosen for consequence. Several are departures from the ranking: Tupac's killer sentenced, Hungary's repeal, the Kyiv strike without warning, the Omar search (Monday, never run), FC Copenhagen for sale, the Volvo recall, MI5, the northern budget demand, and the Black Sea ships.

**Left out.** I dropped Danish supermarkets on alert (rank 4) because it follows yesterday's Danfoss brief and has no decision in it. Deer whistles, a neighbour dispute, two road crashes, a local politician's firm and the Folketing's priests were all dropped for consequence. Greenland's time zone, the helmet proposal, the scooter poll and the Vietnamese visit were dropped for space.

The validator made no splits. One concern: section weight is doing too much here. Two-outlet Danish items outranked world events that six outlets carried.
