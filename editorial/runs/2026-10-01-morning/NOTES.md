## Send-back

The checker struck two stories. In the pig-farming story I had translated "grisefirepart" as
"tripartite", but the body is a four-party one. That struck the short headline, the opening paragraph and
the Gjerding quotation. The copy now names the grisefirepart and glosses it as a four-party body, and
the quotation reads "the four-party body". The short version had said the government "will act" if the
parties fail. Like the opening, it now says what the terms of reference say: the government "is ready to
take the necessary initiatives". The GDP brief called the revision "more than first estimated". Ritzau
says only that it is relative to the latest estimate, published in August, and the lede now says that. I
touched no other story, and the build passes.
