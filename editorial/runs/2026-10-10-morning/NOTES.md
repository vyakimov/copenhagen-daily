# Editorial log: Saturday 10 October 2026, morning edition

**Lead: the 2027 finance bill.** Eight Danish outlets carried the bill's presentation on Friday morning. It is the decision the finance-bill thread has been building towards all week, and it ranked first. Yesterday's lead was the leaked senior-pension cut. Today the whole bill is on the table: defence spending, 10.1 billion kroner in tax cuts, the school money and the SU and senior-pension savings. Ritzau's text via Kristeligt Dagblad is the primary. A second Kristeligt Dagblad piece stays in because it carries Pia Olsen Dyhr's words, which no other source has.
